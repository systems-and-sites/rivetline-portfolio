The build

From a locked AI site to a full GHL system

Started as a site rebuild, grew into funnels, documents and tracking across several workstreams.

  1. Native site rebuild16 pages recreated in the GHL builder from the AI Studio version: editable, per-page SEO, connected to Rivetline's own CRM.
  2. Blog migration20+ posts moved over and matched to the site's header and design.
  3. Site repair and positioningBroken links fixed, H1 structure cleaned up across all 16 pages, missing titles and meta descriptions added.
  4. Fractional Marketing campaignThree pages built around a 10-question interactive diagnostic with results rendered inline.
  5. Live Marketing Dashboard funnelLanding page, gated access page, intake form, and an animated tabbed dashboard demo.
  6. Health Check Result documentA GHL Documents template with merge fields and colour-coded outcome chips.
  7. Two vertical landing pagesHome services and education pages with device mockups, FAQ and Service schema, and lead workflows.

The challenge

The site looked finished. It couldn't be edited.

Chris had built Rivetline's site with GoHighLevel's AI Studio. Every change needed prompts or code, and its form didn't feed his own CRM. I rebuilt it page by page in the native builder, so the team can edit it themselves and every lead lands where it should.

Before

  • AI Studio site, edits by prompt only
  • Form not connected to the sub-account
  • Broken links and missing meta

After

  • Native GHL pages, editable by anyone
  • Forms wired to CRM, tags and pipeline
  • Clean SEO, schema and tracking
